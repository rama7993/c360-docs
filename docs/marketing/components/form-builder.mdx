# **📝 Form Builder**

The **Form Builder** in **C360** allows you to design, manage, and assign forms that can be integrated with campaigns, landing pages, and lead management.

To access it:  
➡️ Go to **Marketing** → **Components** → **Form Builder** from the left-hand menu.

---

## 🗂️ Form Builder Views

- The Forms list provides a complete overview of all created forms.
- Form Builder can be viewed in **Grid View** or **List View**.

<Tabs>

<TabItem value="grid" label="🔲 Grid View" default>

- **Grid View** – Shows each form as a card with quick actions like **edit**.
- Can change the status of the form from **Draft** to **Published** and vice versa.

:::tip

- Hovering on **"i"** icon allows you to see the Description.
- Hoverring on the **Form Card** and clicking the preview icon allows you to see the form preview in a new tab.

  :::

## 🎛️Filtering Forms

You can filter Forms by:

- **Status**
- **Category**
- **Created At**
- **Modified At**
- **All/ My Form / Form Assigned To Me**

:::note

- All: Created by me/Assigned to me/Created by below users.
- My Form: Created by me
- Form Assigned To Me: The form which was assigned to me by someone.
  :::

## ➕ Add Form

- Click **Add Form** to create a new form.
- Fill in the **Form Name**, **Category**, and **Description**.
- Click **Save** to create the form.
- After saving, you will be redirected to the **Form Builder** page where you can add fields and actions.
- Costumize the form by adding fields and defining actions.
- Click on **Save as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Save as Publish** to make it live.

<details>
<summary><strong>Click to see additional actions in builder page</strong></summary>

- Can edit the form name.
- Can preview the form by clicking on the **Preview** button.
- Can **Edit,Copy,Delete and Drag** the form fields.
- On edit field, you can change the **Field Label, Field Type, Placeholder, Required Field, Default Value, Options**.
- Can **Cancel** the form creation process by clicking on **Cancel** button.
- Can search the left panel elements by typing the field name in the search bar.

</details>

:::note 

- On Submit button you can add a **Custom Message** or **Redirect to URL** as submit action.
- On Submit button you can also add ** CRM Submit Actions** like **Modify Campaign Segments, Download an Asset, Send Email to Lead, Send Email to User, Send Form Results**.
:::

## ✏️ Edit Form 

- Select the form you want to edit.
- Click the **Edit** icon.
- Modifications can be made to the form's name, category, description if needed and click **Update**.
- Make the necessary changes in the form builder.
- Click **Update as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Update as Publish** to make it live.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/form-edit.png"
  alt="Edit Form"
  caption="Editing a Form"
/>

## 👁️ Preview Form

- Hover on the form you want to preview in grid view and click on the ***Preview*** button. 
- Or click on the **Preview** icon in the list view.
- A new tab will open with the form preview where you can see the form in desktop, tablet, and mobile views.

## 👥 Assigning Forms 

- Select one or more forms from the list.
- Click **Assign To** at the top.
- Choose a group from the dropdown.
- Click **Assign** to confirm.

## 🗑️ Deleting Form

1. Select the checkbox(es) for the Form you want to delete.
2. Click the **Delete** icon .
3. Click on **Yes** from the confirmation pop-up if you want to delete.
