# 📝 Form Builder

The **Form Builder** in **C360** allows you to design, manage, and assign forms that can be integrated with campaigns, landing pages, and lead management.

To access it:  
➡️ Go to **Marketing** → **Components** → **Form Builder** from the left-hand menu.

---

## 🗂️ Form Builder Views

- The Forms list provides a complete overview of all created forms.
- Form Builder can be viewed in **Grid View** or **List View**.

<Tabs>

<TabItem value="grid" label="🔲 Grid View" default>

- **Grid View** – Shows each form as a card with quick actions like **edit**.
- Can change the status of the form from **Draft** to **Published** and vice versa.

:::tip

- Hovering on **"i"** icon allows you to see the Description.
- Hoverring on the **Form Card** and clicking the preview icon allows you to see the form preview in a new tab.

  :::

###  📝 Classic Form

Classic Form is used to collect information from the users. It can be used for **Lead Generation, Contact Us, Newsletter Subscription** etc.

- Select type as **Classic** and click **Save** to create the form.
- After saving, you will be redirected to the **Form Builder** page where you can add fields and actions.
- Costumize the form by adding fields and defining actions.
- Click on **Save as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Save as Publish** to make it live.

<details>
<summary><strong>Click to see additional actions in builder page</strong></summary>

- Can edit the form name.
- Can preview the form by clicking on the **Preview** button.
- Can **Edit,Copy,Delete and Drag** the form fields.
- On edit field, you can change the **Field Label, Field Type, Placeholder, Required Field, Default Value, Options**.
- Can **Cancel** the form creation process by clicking on **Cancel** button.
- Can search the left panel elements by typing the field name in the search bar.

</details>

:::note 

- On Submit button you can add a **Custom Message** or **Redirect to URL** as submit action.
- On Submit button you can also add ** CRM Submit Actions** like **Modify Campaign Segments, Download an Asset, Send Email to Lead, Send Email to User, Send Form Results**.
:::

### 📋 Survey Form

  Survey Form is used to collect feedback from the users. It can be used for **Customer Satisfaction Survey, Employee Engagement Survey, Product Feedback Survey** etc.

- Select type as **Survey**.
- Survey Form has classified into three types: **Simple Survey, Score Survey, and If/Else survey**.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/create-survey-form.png"
  alt="Create Survey Form"
  caption="Create a New Survey Form"
/>

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="simple survey" label="☑️ Simple Survey">

You can set up sequential multi-step questions without scoring or branching.

- Select type as **Simple Survey** and click **Save** to create the simple survey form.
- After saving, you will be redirected to the **Form Builder** page where you can add fields and actions.
- Click on **Add Page** to add a new page to the survey form.
- Set up the survey form by adding questions and defining actions.
- Click on **Add Ending** to add a new ending to the survey form. 
- Click on **Save as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Save as Publish** to make it live.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/simple-survey.png"
  alt="Simple Survey"
  caption="Create a Simple Survey"
/>

:::note
- You can add only one ending to the simple survey form.
- Endings can be used to route respondents to different results or thank you pages.
:::

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="scoring logic survey" label="⭐ ScorIng Logic Survey">

Assign points for responses and calculate a final score.

- Select type as **Scoring Logic** and click **Save** to create the scoring logic survey form.
- After saving, you will be redirected to the **Form Builder** page where you can add fields and actions under **Workflow** tab.

#### Workflow Tab

- Click on **Add Page** to add a new page to the survey form.
- Set up the survey form by adding questions and defining actions.
- Set the the **Score** for each options of the questions in the scoring survey form.
- Click on **Add Ending** to add  endings to the survey form which you show based on the score. 
- Click on **Condition** tab to set the conditions for the endings based on the score.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/scoring-survey-workflow.png"
  alt="Scoring Survey Workflow"
  caption="Create a Scoring Logic Survey"
/>

:::note
- You can add multiple pages to the scoring survey form.
- You can add multiple endings to the scoring survey form.
- Endings can be used to route respondents to different results or thank you pages.
:::

#### Condition Tab

- Click on **Add Score Rule** to Calculate respondent scores from question choices and route respondents to different endings.
- Set up the Score routing rules.
- Click on **Save as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Save as Publish** to make it live.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/scoring-survey-conditions.png"
  alt="Scoring Survey Conditions"
  caption="Set Conditions for the Scoring Logic Survey"
/>

:::note
- **Endings** are mandatory to set the conditions for the scoring survey form.
- Also you should have set the **Score** for each options of the questions in the scoring survey form before setting the conditions.
- You can add multiple score rules to the scoring survey form.
:::

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="if/else logic survey" label="🔀 If/Else Logic Survey">


Add Score Rule
Configure hierarchical IF/Else rules to route users to different steps based on their answers. If scoring is enabled, option scores are tallied and evaluated.

- Select type as **If/Else Logic** and click **Save** to create the if/else logic survey form.
- After saving, you will be redirected to the **Form Builder** page where you can add fields and actions under **Workflow** tab.

#### Workflow Tab

- Click on **Add Step/Page** to add a new page to the survey form.
- Set up the survey form by adding questions and defining actions.
- Set the the **Score** for each options of the questions in the scoring survey form.
- Click on **Add Ending** to add  endings to the survey form which you show based on the score. 
- Click on **Condition** tab to set the conditions for the endings based on the score.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/if-else-survey-workflow.png"
  alt="If/Else Survey Workflow"
  caption="Create an If/Else Logic Survey"
/>

:::note
- You can add multiple steps/pages to the if/else logic survey form.
- You can add multiple endings to the if/else logic survey form.
- Endings can be used to route respondents to different results or thank you pages.
:::

#### Condition Tab

- Click on ** Add Question Rule** to route users based on their selections with hierarchical question branches.
- Set up the Question routing rules. 
- Additionally you can enable the **Scoring** which is an optional feature and set up the Score routing rules.
- Click on **Save as Draft** to save the form as a draft or **Save as Publish** to make it live.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/if-else-survey-conditions.png"
  alt="If/Else Survey Conditions"
  caption="Set Conditions for the If/Else Logic Survey"
/>

:::note
- Must have multiple questions added in the each steps/pages.
:::

</TabItem>

</Tabs>

:::info
- For **Endings** you can enable **CTA(call to action)** Button.
- You can set the CTA button label text and URL to redirect the respondents to a specific page.
- You can also design and align  CTA button .
:::

## 🔗 Copy Form Link

- Click on the three dots in the  **Grid view** and select **Copy Link**.
- Or click on the **Copy Link** icon in the **List view**.
- You can share the copied link with the respondents to fill the form 
- Or use it inside **Email Template** or **Landing Page** for the elements as **href** links.

<DocImage
  src="/media/marketing/component/form builder/form-copy-link.png"
  alt="Copy Form Link"
  caption="Copying a Form Link"
/>

:::info
- Can copy the link with UTM parameters (Optional).
- The copied link can be shared with the respondents to fill the form will consider as **Stand Alone or External Form**.
- The copied link can be used in the **Email Campaign** or **Landing Page** to redirect the respondents to fill the form is not consider as **Stand Alone or External Form**.
:::
