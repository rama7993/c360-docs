# 📈 Activity Insights

The **Activity Insights** panel provides a quick snapshot of your daily schedule and upcoming actions directly within your workspace. It helps you stay organized without leaving your current view.

## 📋 Features

### 📅 Upcoming Appointments

View your scheduled meetings and appointments for the day or upcoming dates.

- **Date Range Picker**: Filter appointments by selecting a specific date range.
- **Quick View**: See time, subject, and participants at a
  glance.

<DocImage
  src="/media/home/activity-insights/upcoming-appointments.png"
  caption="Upcoming Appointments"
/>

### 📂 Opportunity By Win Probability

Track the likelihood of closing deals in your pipeline.

- **Win Probability Score**: Visual indicator of deal health.
- **Key Metrics**: View stage, value, and expected close date.

<DocImage
  src="/media/home/activity-insights/opportunity-by-win-probability.png"
  caption="Opportunity by win probability"
/>

### 🏆 Top Performing Campaigns

Quickly see which campaigns are driving the most engagement.

- **Open Rate**: Percentage of emails opened.
- **Click Rate**: Percentage of recipients who clicked.
- **Campaign List**: Review specific campaigns with performance metrics.

<DocImage
  src="/media/home/activity-insights/top-performing-campaign.png"
  caption="Top Performing Campaigns"
/>

### 🌟 Top Performing Assets

Track your most popular content pieces.

- **Visits**: Total number of times an asset was viewed.
- **Unique Visits**: Number of distinct users who viewed the asset.

<DocImage
  src="/media/home/activity-insights/top-performing-asset.png"
  caption="Top Performing Assets"
/>

### 📝 Upcoming Tasks

Don't let important to-dos slip through the cracks.

- **Task Card**: Shows the task status and priority.
- **Data Availability**: clear indication if no tasks are pending.

<DocImage
  src="/media/home/activity-insights/upcoming-tasks.png"
  caption="Upcoming Tasks"
/>

## 🚀 How to Access

1.  Look for the **Activity Insights** panel on the right side of your dashboard or workspace.
2.  Expand the panel to view details.
3.  Use the calendar icon to switch date views.

:::tip

Activity Insights is available across most C360 modules, keeping your schedule always visible.
:::
