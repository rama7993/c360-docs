# 💼 **Opportunities**

To access **Opportunities**:  
➡️ Go to **Settings** → **System Configuration** → **Opportunities** from the Top Bar.

---

## 🏷️ Opportunity Type

:::note
Opportunity type is a label assigned to sales prospects, such as _New Business_ or _Renewal_.  
It helps organize and prioritize sales efforts for accurate forecasting and targeted strategies.
:::

### ⚡ Actions

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="add" label="➕ Add" default>
    <strong>Add:</strong> Enter the desired name in the <em>Opportunity Type</em> field and click the <strong>Save</strong> 💾 icon.
  </TabItem>

<TabItem value="edit" label="✏️ Edit">
  <strong>Edit:</strong> Focus on the field, make inline changes, then press{" "}
  <strong>Enter</strong> ⏎ or click outside the field to save.
</TabItem>

  <TabItem value="delete" label="🗑️ Delete">
    <strong>Delete:</strong> Click the <strong>Delete</strong> 🗑️ icon next to the respective opportunity type.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::tip
✅ Once added, these opportunity types appear in the dropdown menu when creating or editing an opportunity.
:::

## 📊 Opportunity Stage

:::note
The **Opportunity Stage** represents the phase of a potential sale, tracking its progress from first contact to deal closure.  
It helps sales teams monitor progress, prioritize efforts, and forecast revenue.
:::

### ⚡ Actions

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="add" label="➕ Add Stage" default>
    <strong>To add a stage:</strong>
    
      1. Enter the stage name.
      1. Open the <strong>color chart</strong> 🎨 and choose a colour.
      1. Click <strong>Select</strong> ✅ to confirm.
      1. Set the <strong>probability value</strong> 📊.
      1. Click <strong>Save</strong> 💾 to store the stage.
    
  
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem value="edit" label="✏️ Edit Stage">
    <strong>To edit a stage:</strong>
    
      1. Update the stage name or probability inline.
      1. Save by pressing <strong>Enter</strong> ⏎ or clicking outside the field.
      1. To update the color, pick a new one 🎨 and confirm.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem value="delete" label="🗑️ Delete Stage">
    <strong>To delete a stage:</strong>
    
      1. Locate the <strong>Delete</strong> 🗑️ button beside the stage.
      1. Click it to remove the stage permanently.
    
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<figure>
  <img
    src="/media/system-configuration/opportunity/opportunity-stage.png"
    alt="Edit Opportunity Stage"
  />
  <figcaption>Editing Opportunity Stage Details</figcaption>
</figure>

:::tip
✅ Stage colors are shown in the **Opportunity List table**, making it easy to visually track stages.
:::

## ❌ Opportunity Loss Reason

:::note
Opportunity Loss Reasons explain why opportunities are marked as **Closed Lost**, helping identify trends and improve sales processes.
:::

### ⚡ Actions

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="add" label="➕ Add" default>
    <strong>To add:</strong>
    
      1. Click <strong>Add</strong> ➕.
      1. Enter the loss reason ✍️.
      1. Click <strong>Save</strong> 💾.
    
  </TabItem>

<TabItem value="edit" label="✏️ Edit">
  <strong>To edit:</strong>
  1. Click on the reason to update it inline ✍️.
  <li>
    Save by pressing <strong>Enter</strong> ⏎ or clicking outside the field.
  </li>
</TabItem>

  <TabItem value="delete" label="🗑️ Delete">
    <strong>To delete:</strong>
    
      1. Locate the <strong>Delete</strong> 🗑️ icon.
      1. Click it to remove the loss reason.
    
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<figure>
  <img
    src="/media/system-configuration/opportunity/opportunity-loss-reason.png"
    alt="Edit Opportunity Loss Reason"
  />
  <figcaption>Editing an Opportunity Loss Reason</figcaption>
</figure>

:::tip
✅ These reasons appear in the **Opportunity Loss Reason dropdown** when setting an opportunity stage to _Closed Lost_.
:::
